# Supplementary Fig. S12 | Paired global loss contrasts and event-level pathway comparisons

a–b, Wind and solar ten-year unit-capacity net-loss differences relative to paired SSP126 in 2030–2039, 2040–2049 and 2050–2059. Ocher squares show SSP245−SSP126 and red triangles SSP585−SSP126. Large symbols show four-model means, capped bars the model minimum–maximum, and small open circles individual model differences. Differences are calculated within each model before averaging. Left-to-right model offsets are CANESM5, MPI-ESM1-2-HR, MRI-ESM2-0 and BCC-CSM2-MR. These are the original Fig. 2 c–d.

c–d, Annualized event-specific unit-capacity net losses in 2050–2059. Circles, squares and triangles show four-model means for SSP126, SSP245 and SSP585; grey segments connect SSP126 and SSP585 and do not denote time evolution. Events retain the original descending SSP126 meteorological-exposure order. These are the original Fig. 2 e–f. Overlapping event labels cannot be summed to recover event-union losses.

All axes use MWh MW⁻¹ yr⁻¹. Climate and deployment SSPs are paired; their contrasts include both changes. Signed model values and original capacity denominators are retained, without wind rescaling. Common-support rules are the same as Fig. 2. Four-model ranges are descriptive spread, not confidence intervals. station_ssp585 originates from the SSP5-6.0 deployment file.

Source data are panel_ab_models.csv and panel_ab_display.csv for paired differences, panel_cd_models.csv and panel_cd_display.csv for event losses, and capacity_coverage.csv. Input and output hashes are retained in data_audit.json and metadata.json. The complete four-panel PNG and independent a+b and c+d PNGs use 600 dpi.
