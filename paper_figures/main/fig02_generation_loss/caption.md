# Fig. 2 | Global net generation losses across paired pathways

a–b, Global annual signed net loss per unit installed capacity in wind and solar, 2030–2059 (MWh MW⁻¹ yr⁻¹). Lines and annual symbols are the equally weighted means of four climate models; shaded bands show the model minimum–maximum, not confidence intervals. Each pathway pairs its climate SSP with its deployment SSP. The 2030, 2040 and 2050 capacity snapshots apply to 2030–2039, 2040–2049 and 2050–2059 respectively. Lines and bands are disconnected at snapshot changes; dotted vertical lines mark 2040 and 2050. Native-calendar annual aggregates are retained without imposing a common number of hours.

c–d, Ten-year mean differences from the paired SSP126 pathway, computed within each model before ensemble aggregation. Ocher squares compare SSP245 with SSP126, red triangles SSP585 with SSP126. Large symbols are four-model means, bars are model minimum–maximum, and small open circles show individual models. Within each pathway group, model points run left to right as CANESM5, MPI-ESM1-2-HR, MRI-ESM2-0 and BCC-CSM2-MR; horizontal offsets only separate symbols. Each window contains ten annual observations per model; these are not forty independent climate realizations. No significance test is applied.

e–f, Event-labelled annual net losses in 2050–2059, averaged within model and then equally across four models. Circles, squares and triangles denote paired SSP126, SSP245 and SSP585 pathways. Grey segments connect SSP126 and SSP585; they do not describe temporal transitions. Event rows follow descending SSP126 capacity-weighted meteorological exposure, matching the Fig. 1 ordering rule. Both technologies retain their complete published event lists. Event labels can overlap and cannot be added to recover losses during the union of events. Model-resolved event values remain in panel_ef.csv.

All metrics are ratios of signed loss energy summed over valid stations to capacity summed over those same stations. Event denominators contain the full valid installed capacity, including stations with zero event loss. Wind energy is used at its original scale. Negative net losses remain signed; absent observations or absent capacity are not replaced with zero. All displayed model/pathway/year/event combinations are available. Technologies share axis ranges within each row.

The accepted common support requires finite annual fields across all selected models, climates, events and ten years within each deployment snapshot. Capacity can differ between pathways and snapshots. This annual validity rule does not establish identical valid three-hourly timestamps. Capacity coverage relative to the station catalogue is reported in data_audit.json and the complete model/pathway/snapshot denominators in source_data/capacity_coverage.csv. Paired-pathway differences include both climate and deployment changes. station_ssp585 originates from the SSP5-6.0 station file and is an independent deployment dimension.

Sources: source_data/panel_ab.csv (720 annual records), panel_cd.csv (24 model-resolved paired-contrast records), panel_ef.csv (132 model/event/pathway records); panel_cd_models.csv supplements explicit target/reference identities and denominators. Display summaries, ordering exposure and coverage are retained in the same directory. data_audit.json records input hashes, validity checks and event ordering; metadata.json records code/source/PNG hashes, axis ranges, dimensions and job ID.
