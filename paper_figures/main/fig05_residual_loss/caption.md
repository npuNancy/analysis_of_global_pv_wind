# Fig. 5 | Residual loss under SSP126 and paired-path differences

a, Wind. b, Solar PV. Each circle is one country in the fixed early/late comparable population (147 wind; 149 solar PV). Coordinates are equal-weight means of four GCMs: CANESM5, MPI-ESM1-2-HR, MRI-ESM2-0 and BCC-CSM2-MR. The x axis gives annual signed net loss per installed MW under climate SSP126 and deployment SSP126 in 2050–2059. The y axis gives within-model losses under paired SSP585 minus paired SSP126 climate/deployment paths, averaged across models. Both coordinates use MWh MW⁻¹ yr⁻¹.

Circle area is proportional to country catalogue capacity under SSP126 in the 2050 snapshot (GW), using one scale across panels. Red/blue circles require at least three of four model differences to agree with the nonzero ensemble direction. Hollow grey circles indicate unclear direction. Agreement is not statistical significance. Crosses show four model results for USA and China; these indicate model spread, not independent annual samples or confidence intervals. Country labels reuse the eight Fig. 4 explanation cases.

Dashed vertical lines mark the pre-established country-equal 75th percentile of four-model mean SSP126 loss in 2030–2039: 129.45 for wind and 96.76 for solar PV. Each technology uses its fixed early/late comparable population. Relative high loss requires loss above its threshold and above zero; these are not reliability limits. The horizontal line marks zero paired-path difference.

Losses use the all-event temporal union: sums of signed net loss divided by capacity on common valid station support within each deployment, followed by ten-year averaging. No wind rescaling is applied. Missing comparisons, absent reference capacity and countries outside the fixed population are recorded in excluded_countries.csv and are never zero-filled. China includes the merged China and Taiwan analysis geometries. The source station SSP5-6.0 dataset maps to the formal SSP585 deployment label. Annual valid-capacity coverage by model, paired scenario, country and year is in coverage.csv; window numerators and denominators are in paired_windows.csv.

Both axes share the SSP126 loss term and are mathematically coupled. This is descriptive classification, with no regression or causal inference. Residual losses are not asserted to be unavoidable. Source data and numerical validation are in outputs/source_data/ and metadata.json.

The minimum common-valid country catalogue-capacity coverage is 78.34% for wind and 79.71% for solar PV. The source input contains 162 wind and 167 solar country records: 13 wind and 17 solar countries lack complete paired losses; two further wind countries (LBN, MNE) and one solar country (ALB) are outside the pre-established early/late population. The final panels retain 147 and 149 countries respectively. All exclusions remain in source_data/excluded_countries.csv.
